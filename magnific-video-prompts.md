# Prompts para Magnific — NOWHERE / Casa El Diablo

Modelo recomendado: Seedance 2.5 dentro de Magnific.

> Nota de actualización: estos prompts conceptuales anteriores ya no corresponden a la tanda activa. La nueva dirección usa exclusivamente las referencias `hero-burger-poster.png`, `restaurant-discovery.png` y `poster-wall.png`: burger con vapor y chisporroteo, llegada nocturna al restaurante y paneo lateral por los pósters NOWHERE. La web no utiliza los videos anteriores.

Los tres clips forman una secuencia narrativa: **lo imagina → lo descubre → lo vive**. Mantener 16:9, 8 segundos, sin diálogo, sin texto generado dentro del vídeo y con una estética coherente: negro carbón, marfil envejecido, rojo óxido, misterio editorial y movimiento de cámara lento.

## Clip 01 — El sueño (2223/10.000 caracteres)

```text
[SCENE CONTEXT]
8-second, 16:9, one continuous take. A surreal monochrome dream suggests a hidden restaurant before it is revealed. Final state: the abstract eye-like form becomes still, leaving a warm point of light in the darkness.

[LOCATION MAP]
Abstract black space with faint paper grain. A pale circular form floats in the upper-right area. Thin ivory lines cross the frame diagonally. A small rust-red iris sits inside an almond-shaped outline. No people, no readable signs, no food.

[FIRST FRAME AND SPATIAL BLOCKING]
The first frame is nearly black with only a dim ivory arc and a distant rust-red point. The circular form is off-center to the right, leaving negative space on the left for later web copy. Keep the composition sparse and graphic.

[FORMAT MODE]
One continuous take, 8 seconds, real time with a subtle dreamlike slow motion.

[OPTICS]
Wide field of view, shallow atmospheric depth, soft focus around the edges, no lens distortion.

[CAMERA]
Start static. Slowly push forward toward the pale circular form while drifting two degrees clockwise. End in a restrained hold on the rust-red iris. No cuts, no handheld shake, no sudden zoom.

[TIMELINE / BEATS]
0.0–2.0s: The dim arc breathes almost imperceptibly; paper grain moves like dust in a projector beam.
2.0–5.5s: The pale circle expands slightly as the diagonal lines slide across it; the rust-red iris opens and closes once, slowly.
5.5–8.0s: The circle settles, the iris becomes a small steady ember, and the image holds.

[LIGHTING]
Low-key black background, soft ivory glow, one muted rust accent. No neon, no glossy 3D, no hard highlights.

[AUDIO]
No dialogue. Low room tone, distant air movement, one soft tonal pulse when the iris closes. No music with a beat.

[STYLE AND QUALITY]
Editorial art film, monochrome psychological-test reference, tactile paper grain, restrained analog imperfection, premium restaurant campaign mood.

[POSITIVE LOCKS]
Sparse composition. One abstract eye-like form only. Stable left-side negative space. Slow controlled camera. Rust-red accent remains subtle.

[NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS]
No readable text, logos, faces, extra eyes, duplicated objects, jump cuts, rapid motion, fantasy creatures, or watermark.
```

## Clip 02 — El descubrimiento (2491/10.000 caracteres)

```text
[SCENE CONTEXT]
8-second, 16:9, one continuous take. A remote house-like restaurant appears across an open night landscape, turning an unknown place into a real destination. Final state: one warm window remains visible beneath the moon.

[LOCATION MAP]
Open dark landscape with low angular mountains. A small isolated house-like restaurant stands slightly right of center. A pale moon is high on the right. A narrow road leads from the lower-left foreground toward the building. No people, no cars, no readable signs.

[FIRST FRAME AND SPATIAL BLOCKING]
The first frame shows the moon behind thin cloud and the building almost hidden in the valley. Keep generous dark negative space on the left for later web copy. The building has exactly three warm rectangular windows.

[FORMAT MODE]
One continuous take, 8 seconds, slow real-time movement.

[OPTICS]
35mm cinematic perspective, moderate depth of field, foreground road slightly soft, building readable but distant.

[CAMERA]
Start low over the road. Make a slow forward tracking move with a gentle rise, keeping the building in the right third and the moon above it. End with a quiet hold. No cuts, no orbit, no reversed screen direction.

[TIMELINE / BEATS]
0.0–2.5s: A thin cloud passes the moon; the road texture becomes visible as the camera advances.
2.5–5.5s: The camera rises and the isolated building emerges from shadow; three windows warm gradually, one after another.
5.5–8.0s: The camera stops. The building, road, mountains and moon hold in a composed tableau.

[PHYSICS AND MOTION CADENCE]
Cloud moves slowly with natural wind. Road dust remains grounded. Window light changes softly without flicker. Mountains and building remain fixed.

[LIGHTING]
Deep charcoal sky, pale ivory moonlight, muted rust-orange window light, strong but soft silhouettes, no artificial lens flare.

[AUDIO]
No dialogue. Distant night wind, faint gravel under a slow vehicle-like camera movement, one subtle low impact when the lights appear. No vocals.

[STYLE AND QUALITY]
Editorial mystery campaign, cinematic landscape illustration with realistic atmospheric depth, restrained grain, dark warm palette, premium and quiet.

[POSITIVE LOCKS]
Exactly one isolated building. Exactly three warm windows. Road leads toward the building. Moon stays upper-right. Keep left-side negative space.

[NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS]
No extra buildings, people, cars, signs, readable text, fireworks, city skyline, duplicated windows, sudden weather, or watermark.
```

## Clip 03 — La decisión (2644/10.000 caracteres)

```text
[SCENE CONTEXT]
8-second, 16:9, one continuous take. A first-person visitor reaches toward a dark restaurant door and opens it, turning curiosity into a lived experience. Final state: warm interior light spills into the frame and the door is partly open.

[LOCATION MAP]
Old dark wood door fills the right half of the frame. Narrow warm light leaks around its edges. The camera is the visitor's eye level, close to the entrance. One human hand enters from the lower center and reaches toward a small metal handle. No face, no logo, no readable sign.

[FIRST FRAME AND SPATIAL BLOCKING]
The first frame shows the closed door in near darkness, with a thin rust-colored glow under the frame. The handle is at right-center. The hand is not yet visible. Preserve clear dark negative space on the left for web copy.

[FORMAT MODE]
One continuous take, 8 seconds, real time with a slight final hold.

[OPTICS]
50mm perspective at eye level, shallow depth of field on the handle and hand, soft falloff into darkness.

[CAMERA]
Start still. Make a subtle forward move as the hand enters and reaches. Follow the hand by a few centimeters, then stop when the door opens. Keep the door axis stable; no camera rotation or cut.

[TIMELINE / BEATS]
0.0–2.0s: Closed door, quiet darkness, a thin warm line under the threshold.
2.0–4.5s: A single hand enters from below, fingers extend, contact the handle and close around it.
4.5–6.5s: The handle turns; the door opens inward with visible weight and a small pause at the hinge.
6.5–8.0s: Warm amber interior light spills across the floor and hand; hold on the partly open door.

[PHYSICS AND MOTION CADENCE]
The hand makes one clear contact. The handle turns before the door moves. The door has weight, hinge resistance and a small settling motion. Warm light persists in the final frame.

[LIGHTING]
Near-black exterior, warm amber light from inside, muted rust highlights on the hand and wood, soft contact shadows, no blown-out white.

[AUDIO]
No dialogue. Quiet room tone, one small metal handle click, soft wood hinge sound, then a low interior ambience as the light appears. No music beat.

[STYLE AND QUALITY]
Editorial restaurant campaign, tactile wood and skin texture, cinematic realism with restrained grain, intimate first-person discovery, premium and mysterious.

[POSITIVE LOCKS]
One hand only. One door only. Handle contact precedes opening. Door opens inward. Warm interior light persists. Left side remains dark and uncluttered.

[NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS]
No face, second hand, extra person, readable text, logo, door morphing, floating hand, duplicated handle, sudden light burst, or watermark.
```

## Inserción en la web

Guardar los archivos generados con estos nombres dentro de `videos/`:

- `nowhere-dream.mp4`
- `nowhere-discovery.mp4`
- `nowhere-decision.mp4`

Después se pueden insertar como fondos `<video autoplay muted loop playsinline>` en las tres escenas correspondientes. Los fondos animados CSS actuales funcionan como fallback mientras Magnific no entregue los archivos.
